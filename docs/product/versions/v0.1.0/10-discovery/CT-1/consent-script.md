# Consent Script

Read before the session and record only `consent: true` or `false` with the participant ID.

We are evaluating an independently implemented, self-hosted project-management prototype. We are testing the product and the proposed workflow, not your ability. The session takes about 50 minutes and uses synthetic data only.

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any time. Please do not open an employer system, share a screen containing private work, or enter names, issues, customer data, credentials, or other confidential information.

By default, the session is not audio- or video-recorded. The moderator will take anonymized notes under a participant ID. Repository findings will not include your name, employer, email address, account identifiers, or verbatim quotes. Private scheduling details remain separate from research notes.

You may ask to withdraw your session before synthesis by providing your participant ID. Raw private notes will be deleted after synthesis plus seven days; the anonymized aggregate matrix may be retained as project evidence.

The prototype may contain defects and should not be used for real work. Your participation does not grant the project permission to contact you for unrelated purposes.

Do you understand this and consent to participate under these conditions?

Record:

- participant ID;
- consent yes/no;
- recording yes/no, where no is the default;
- date;
- moderator ID.

Do not continue when consent is no.
