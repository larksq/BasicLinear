# Participant Screener

Use this screener verbally or in a private form. Do not place contact details or employer names in repository artifacts.

## Required Eligibility

1. Are you at least 18 years old and able to consent to a 50-minute product-research session?
2. Do you currently work in product management, software engineering, engineering management, product design, technical program management, or closely related product delivery work?
3. Does the product and engineering group that shares your issue-tracking workflow have 3 to 50 regular collaborators?
4. Have you used an issue or project tracker at least weekly during the last three months?
5. Does your team face at least one material constraint involving source openness, self-hosting, data residency, software cost, extensibility, or vendor independence?
6. Can you evaluate a desktop web prototype using entirely synthetic data without opening your employer's systems?
7. Are you independent of this project's sponsor and implementation contributors?

An eligible participant answers yes to all seven questions.

## Classification

Record only these categories:

- role band: product, engineering individual contributor, engineering manager, design or research, technical program or operations;
- collaborator band: 3-10, 11-20, 21-50;
- tracker familiarity: weekly, several-times-weekly, daily;
- hard constraint: openness, self-hosting, residency, cost, extensibility, independence, multiple;
- tooling influence: user, recommender, decision maker, operator;
- self-hosting experience: none, trial only, regular operator;
- current tracker category: commercial issue tracker, repository-native tracker, open-source tracker, mixed workflow.

## Exclusions

Exclude anyone who must reveal confidential employer data, cannot avoid logging into a real workspace, has materially contributed to the prototype, is evaluating on behalf of a competitor under a confidentiality restriction, or cannot provide informed consent.

## Quota Check

Before the fifth session, verify that the sample includes the role, team-size, influence, and tracker-context coverage in [research-plan.md](./research-plan.md). Recruit additional participants rather than relaxing eligibility after seeing results.
