# Recruitment Brief

Status: target list and outreach authorization required

## Audience

Product managers, software engineers, engineering managers, product designers, and technical program or operations practitioners in three-to-fifty-person product and engineering groups who use issue trackers weekly and face a real openness, self-hosting, residency, cost, extensibility, or vendor-independence constraint.

## Outreach Copy

Subject: 50-minute research session on self-hosted project tracking

We are researching an independently implemented, open-source project-management tool for small product and engineering teams. We are looking for people who use an issue tracker weekly and have a real constraint involving self-hosting, data control, cost, extensibility, source availability, or vendor independence.

The 50-minute session uses synthetic data in a prototype. You will not need to log into your employer's systems or share confidential information. The session covers your current workflow, a short set of project and issue tasks, migration blockers, and deployment constraints.

Participation is voluntary. We use anonymized notes and do not record by default. Reply only if you would like the private eligibility screener and scheduling options.

## Operational Boundary

Do not send outreach through Gmail, Calendar, social accounts, or mailing lists until the sponsor supplies or approves the recipients, incentive, sender identity, time zone, and available session windows. Existing account access is capability, not permission to contact people.

Recruitment contact data stays outside the repository and is never entered into Control Tower descriptions. Control Tower records only counts, anonymized participant IDs, status, evidence paths, and decisions.
