# Interview Guide

Use neutral follow-ups. Ask for a recent concrete example without requesting confidential details.

## Current Work

1. What role do you play in planning and executing product or engineering work?
2. How often do you create, update, search, or review issues and projects?
3. Walk me through the last planning or execution task that felt slower or less trustworthy than it should have. What happened next?
4. Which workflow objects do you use daily, weekly, monthly, or rarely?

Prompt only after the open answer: projects, milestones or cycles, issues, sub-issues, relations, comments, activity, saved views, search, imports, exports, and backup or restore.

## Constraint and Urgency

5. Which openness, hosting, residency, cost, extensibility, or vendor-independence constraint is real for your team today?
6. Has that constraint caused a failed procurement, blocked adoption, policy exception, migration attempt, or deadline? When?
7. What event would cause you to trial a replacement in the next three months?
8. What would make the constraint tolerable enough that you would not switch?

## Migration and Operations

9. Which records, relations, history, attachments, identities, and settings must survive a trial migration?
10. Who would deploy and operate a self-hosted tool? What deployment format is acceptable?
11. Which identity, backup, upgrade, observability, integration, or support requirement blocks a trial?
12. What is the maximum credible setup and maintenance burden?

## Alternatives

13. What do you use or combine today, and what is the best available alternative?
14. Why have you not already moved to that alternative?
15. After the prototype tasks, which would you choose for a bounded team trial: this concept, your current workflow, a named substitute, or no change? Why?

Do not ask the participant to open or operate any competitor during the session.

## Forced Ranking

Ask the participant to rank these from most to least important for a first trial:

- project planning;
- ordered milestones and progress;
- issue capture and property editing;
- issue relations and sub-issues;
- comments and activity history;
- saved filtered views;
- workspace search and command navigation;
- archive and restore;
- export and backup restore;
- optional OIDC;
- multi-team support;
- attachments;
- real-time collaboration.

Record the top five and bottom three. Then ask: what is missing that should replace one of the top five?

## Closing

16. What would prevent you from trusting this product with a real trial?
17. What is the smallest evidence that would change your mind?
18. Which assumption in our proposed segment or scope sounds most wrong?
19. May we contact you for one follow-up about this study only?
