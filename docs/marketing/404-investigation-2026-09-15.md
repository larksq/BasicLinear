## Findings

The 79 requests attributed to Vercel's `/404.html` route reconcile exactly by original request path. They do not represent 79 failed application visits or 79 unique people.

Independent Vercel bot categories: `browser_impersonation` 46, `scanner` 18, unclassified 9, `http_client` 3, `ai_assistant` 1, `ai_crawler` 1, `webhook` 1. These categories total 79 and overlap the path classifications above; they must not be added to them. A bot label is provider attribution, not proof of a specific actor or intent.

The hosted UI uses the root/`hosted.html` entry and hash-based issue links. The historical 404 route contains no current generated hosted JS/CSS/font filename or supported application entry. There is no evidence here of a missing SPA deep-link rewrite.
